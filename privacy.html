Muse

Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

In short: Muse runs on your own PC. It reads your Gmail and Google Calendar only if you connect them, and only read-only. Your data stays on your PC, except what goes to the AI agent you choose to run. The people who make Muse never receive it, and Muse has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Who this covers

This policy covers Muse, a desktop app for Windows made by james-glen-uk. Muse has no servers and no accounts of its own. Everything it does happens on the PC it's installed on.

Google data Muse can read

Connecting Google is optional. If you connect it, Muse asks Google for two read-only permissions:

  • Gmail, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): the sender, subject, date and text of your emails.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly): your events, with their times, titles, places, organisers, attendees, descriptions and meeting links.

Muse can't send, delete, label or change any email, and can't create, change or delete any event.

How Muse uses it

  • To show today's meetings on Home.
  • To find to-dos and updates in your inbox when you select Refresh.
  • To let your AI agents look up your mail and calendar when you ask them a question that needs it.

Muse uses Google data only for these features, which you can see and control in the app. It doesn't use Google data for advertising, doesn't sell it, and doesn't use it to train AI models.

Where it's kept

  • Your Google sign-in is saved on your PC, encrypted by Windows for your user account. So is the client secret of the Google Cloud project you connect with.
  • Things Muse makes from your mail and calendar, such as to-dos, updates and chats with your agents, are saved as files on your PC, in %APPDATA%\Muse and your Muse folder.
  • Nothing is uploaded to the people who make Muse. There is nowhere to upload it to.

Who it's shared with

Muse talks to Google directly from your PC to read your mail and calendar. Beyond Google, it shares your data only with the AI agent you choose to run, and only the parts that agent needs to do what you asked:

  • If you run a cloud AI service, such as Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Grok, Kimi or OpenRouter, the relevant email or calendar text is sent to that service, using your own account or API key. That service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • If you run a local model through Ollama, your data stays on your PC.

Muse doesn't share Google data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Muse's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google: in Muse, open Settings → Integrations → Google and select Disconnect. Muse deletes your sign-in and tells Google to cancel it.
  • Remove access from Google's side: go to myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose Muse and remove its access.
  • Delete everything: uninstall Muse, then delete the %APPDATA%\Muse folder and your Muse folder.

Children

Muse isn't meant for children under 13.
