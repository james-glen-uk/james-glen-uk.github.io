Muse

Your personal AI OS for Windows

Muse is a desktop app that brings your AI agents into one place and gives them a Home that knows your day.

What Muse does

  • Runs the AI agents you already use, such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini and local models through Ollama, side by side.
  • Keeps your chats and workspaces as files on your own PC.
  • Shows a Home page with your day, your top to-dos and new updates.

Gmail and Google Calendar

If you choose to connect Google, Muse asks for read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses this to show today's meetings, to find to-dos and updates in your inbox when you ask it to, and to let your agents answer questions about your mail and calendar.

Muse can't send, delete or change any email or event. It has no servers of its own: your Google data goes from Google to Muse on your PC, and from there only to the AI agent you choose to run.

Read the privacy policy for the details.

Who makes Muse

Muse is a personal project by james-glen-uk on GitHub. Each person who uses it connects Google through their own Google Cloud project.
